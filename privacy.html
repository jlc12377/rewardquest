Privacy

How we handle your family's data.

Last updated: May 30, 2026

RewardQuest is a family habit-tracking app designed to be set up and managed by a parent or legal guardian. We take your family's privacy seriously, especially when it comes to children. This page explains exactly what we collect, why, and what your rights are.

The short version: A parent signs up first, creates a family, and invites their child by sharing a private family code. We collect only what's necessary to make the app work. Parents can see and delete their child's data at any time. We never sell data, never show ads, and never share information with third parties for marketing.

1. Who we are

RewardQuest is an independent family app operated by an individual parent-developer based in Tennessee, United States. You can reach us at oliviahopeslife@gmail.com for any privacy questions, data requests, or concerns.

2. Who can use the app

RewardQuest is intended for use by families. Only a parent or legal guardian (age 18 or older) may create an account. Children may only join a family that a parent has already created, and only by entering the family code that the parent shares with them.

We do not allow children to create their own accounts without parental involvement. The act of a parent creating a family, generating a family code, and sharing that code with their child constitutes the parent's verifiable consent to their child's use of the app and to our collection of the child's data as described below.

3. What we collect

From parents

  • Email address (for sign-in and account recovery)
  • Password (stored encrypted; we never see it in plaintext)
  • Optionally, the parent's display name or avatar emoji if they choose to set one
  • Records of approval actions the parent takes within the app

From children

  • Email address and password (for the child's sign-in; provided by the parent during setup)
  • A first name or display name (parent's choice)
  • An avatar emoji the child selects
  • Photos or short videos the child uploads as proof of completing tasks
  • Records of tasks completed, choices logged, and rewards redeemed
  • Limited technical data (IP address, device type, app version) for security and to make the app function

What we don't collect

  • We do not collect children's real names beyond what the parent chooses to enter
  • We do not collect home addresses, phone numbers, or geolocation
  • We do not use third-party advertising networks
  • We do not use third-party analytics that track individual users
  • We do not collect biometric data
  • We do not access contacts, calendar, or other device data beyond what's needed for the app to function

4. How we use the data

We use the information we collect only to operate the app for your family:

  • To authenticate parent and child sign-ins
  • To display the family's tasks, rewards, points, and history within the app
  • To deliver photos and videos that the child submits to the parent for approval
  • To send transactional emails like password resets when requested
  • To maintain security and prevent abuse of the service

We do not use any data for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose beyond running the app itself.

5. Who can see the data

Within a family, the parent who created the family can see all data associated with their children in that family. Children can see only their own data. No one outside the family can see your family's data.

We use Supabase as our database and authentication provider. Supabase has access to the data for the technical purpose of providing the database service, under their own privacy commitments. We do not share data with any other third parties.

6. Your rights as a parent

As a parent or guardian, you have full control over your child's data:

  • Review: You can view all of your child's data through the parent dashboard within the app, including every photo and video submitted, every reward redeemed, and every task logged.
  • Delete: You can delete individual items (photos, claims, redemptions) from the parent dashboard, or delete all of your child's data at any time.
  • Revoke consent: You can close your child's account at any time by emailing us at oliviahopeslife@gmail.com. We will delete all associated data within 30 days.
  • Stop further collection: At any time, you may sign your child out of the app and no further data will be collected.

7. How long we keep data

We keep data only as long as your family is actively using the app. If a family stops using the app for an extended period (12 months of inactivity), we may delete the family's data automatically. You can request immediate deletion at any time by contacting us.

When you delete an item from the dashboard, it is permanently removed from our systems within a reasonable period (typically immediately, but no later than 7 days).

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect family data:

  • All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections
  • Passwords are stored using industry-standard password hashing (never in plaintext)
  • Uploaded photos and videos are stored in a secure private bucket with restricted access
  • Database access is restricted by role-based security policies

No internet service is perfectly secure, and we will notify affected families promptly if we ever experience a data breach involving personal information.

9. Compliance with COPPA

This service complies with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). The parent's act of creating a family account, generating a unique family invite code, and sharing that code with their child constitutes verifiable parental consent under COPPA. We do not allow any child to enter the app without first being invited by a parent who has set up the family and explicitly agreed to this Privacy Policy.

If you have COPPA-related questions or wish to exercise any rights described above, please contact us at oliviahopeslife@gmail.com.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to how we handle your family's data, we will update this page and notify parents through the app or by email before the changes take effect.

11. Contact

Questions, concerns, data requests, or anything else: oliviahopeslife@gmail.com
